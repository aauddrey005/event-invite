# event-invite# Event Invite

![screenshot](screenshot.png)

**Live demo:** [audrey-invite-demo.netlify.app]([[https://audrey-invite-demo.netlify.app)]

A two-page event invitation website built from scratch in HTML and CSS, and my first site I designed, built and put online myself.

---

## Why I built it
I wanted a digital invitation that felt like a printed card, warm, elegant, and personal, instead of a plain group-chat message. It was also my chance to learn how a website is actually made, after working with one from the business side during my e-commerce internship.

## What I built
- **Two pages:** the invitation, and a thank-you page reached from the RSVP button.
- **Card-style design:** cream, coffee and brown colours, with serif and script fonts from Google Fonts.
- **Hand-placed illustrations:** 10 inline SVG drawings (the cake, sparkles and decorations), so they stay sharp at any size.
- **Accessibility:** the button's animation turns off for visitors who've set "reduce motion" on their device.
- **Deployed live** on Netlify.

## What was tricky
[e.g. getting everything centred and spaced like a real card / making the SVGs sit in the right place / getting it to look right on a phone]

- How to take a site from files on my laptop to a live link anyone can open.
